Your billing settings control your subscription plan, payment method, and invoices. Access them by going to **Settings → Billing** (Owner-only access).

<Note>Only the workspace Owner can view and manage billing settings.</Note>

## Viewing your current plan

Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan** to see:
- Your current subscription tier
- Next renewal date and amount
- Usage summary (members, storage, etc.) relative to your plan limits

## Upgrading your plan

To unlock more features or increase limits:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Click **Upgrade Plan**.
3. Compare available plans and click **Select** on the one you want.
4. Confirm the pricing and click **Confirm Upgrade**.

Your new plan takes effect immediately and you'll be billed a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle.

## Downgrading your plan

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Click **Change Plan** and select a lower tier.
3. Review what features and limits will change.
4. Confirm the downgrade.

The downgrade takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle — you keep access to your current plan until then.

<Warning>Downgrading may remove members, reduce storage, or disable features currently in use. Review the impact before confirming.</Warning>

## Updating your payment method

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Method**.
2. Click **Update Payment Method**.
3. Enter your new card details.
4. Click **Save**.

Your new payment method will be used for the next billing cycle and all future charges.

## Viewing and downloading invoices

All past invoices are stored in **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Click any invoice to:
- View the full breakdown
- Download it as a PDF
- Forward it to an email address

## Cancelling your subscription

To cancel:

1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
3. Select a cancellation reason (optional) and confirm.

Your account remains active until the end of the current billing period. After that, it switches to the free tier (if available) or becomes read-only.

<Tip>Before cancelling, export your data from **Settings → Data & Privacy → Export** so you have a copy of all your work.</Tip>

## Billing FAQs

<Accordion title="When am I charged?">
You're charged on the same date each month (or year, for annual plans) — the anniversary of when you first subscribed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get a refund?">
Refund eligibility depends on your plan and region. Contact [support](/support/contact) within 14 days of a charge to request a review.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if my payment fails?">
If a payment fails, you'll receive an email notification. The app will retry the charge automatically for up to 7 days. If it still fails, your account may be downgraded to the free tier until a valid payment method is added.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or educational institutions?">
Yes — contact [support](/support/contact) with proof of your status to apply for a discount.
</Accordion>
