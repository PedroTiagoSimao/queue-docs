Your profile is how other workspace members see you in the app. Keeping it up to date ensures teammates can identify you, mention you correctly, and contact you when needed.

## Accessing your profile settings

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner of the app.
2. Select **Profile Settings** from the dropdown menu.

Or navigate directly to **Settings → Profile**.

## What you can update

### Display name
Your display name appears on all items you create or comment on, and in member lists. To change it, type your new name in the **Display Name** field and click **Save**.

### Profile photo
Your photo appears next to your name throughout the app. To update it:

1. Click your current photo or the placeholder avatar on the Profile Settings page.
2. Select **Upload photo**.
3. Choose a JPEG or PNG file (max 5 MB).
4. Crop and position the image, then click **Apply**.

### Bio
Add a short bio (up to 160 characters) to tell teammates about your role or expertise. This appears on your profile card when others hover over your name.

### Contact and social links
Optionally add links to help teammates reach you:
- Email address (shown to workspace members)
- LinkedIn, GitHub, or other profile URL

## How your profile appears to others

When a teammate hovers over your name in the app, they see a **profile card** showing:
- Your display name and photo
- Your bio
- Your current online status (if enabled)
- Any contact links you've added

You can control your visibility in [Privacy Settings](/guides/settings#privacy-controls).

## Changing your email address

Your email address is used for login and notifications. To change it:

1. Go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Click **Change Email**.
3. Enter your new email address and your current password to confirm.
4. A verification link is sent to the new address — click it to complete the change.

<Note>Until you verify the new email, your old email address remains active for login.</Note>

## Changing your display language

To change the language the app uses in its interface, go to **Settings → Language & Region** and select your preferred language from the dropdown.
