## What you can do with the app

The app is designed to help you work faster and smarter. Here are some of the things you can do:

- **Manage your work** — Create, organize, and track tasks or items in one place.
- **Stay connected** — Receive real-time notifications and updates so nothing slips through.
- **Customize your experience** — Adjust settings, themes, and preferences to fit your workflow.
- **Integrate with other tools** — Connect the apps you already use to keep everything in sync.
- **Collaborate with others** — Share, comment, and work alongside your team.
