Setting up your account correctly from the start makes the app work better for you. This guide covers all the essentials: your profile, security, notifications, and how to tailor the app to your needs.

## Step 1 — Fill in your profile

Your profile is how others on the platform recognize you. To update it:

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Profile Settings**.
3. Add or update your display name, bio, and profile photo.
4. Click **Save changes**.

<Tip>Use a clear photo and your real name so teammates and collaborators can identify you easily.</Tip>

## Step 2 — Set a secure password

If you signed up with an email and password, make sure your password meets the security requirements:

- At least 8 characters
- Contains a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- Includes at least one number or special character

To change your password, go to **Account Settings → Security → Change Password**.

## Step 3 — Enable two-factor authentication

For extra account security, enable two-factor authentication (2FA):

1. Go to **Account Settings → Security**.
2. Click **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy).
4. Enter the verification code to confirm.

<Warning>Save your backup codes in a safe place. You'll need them if you lose access to your authenticator app.</Warning>

## Step 4 — Configure notifications

Choose how and when the app notifies you:

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Toggle the notification types you want to receive (email, in-app, push).
3. Set your preferred notification schedule to avoid alerts during off hours.

See the [Notifications guide](/guides/notifications) for the full list of options.

## Step 5 — Connect your integrations

If you use other tools in your workflow, connect them now:

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations**.
2. Browse the available integrations and click **Connect** next to the ones you use.
3. Authorize the connection when prompted.

See the [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview) for details on each supported integration.

## You're all set

Your account is now configured and ready to use. Head to the [Quick Start](/quickstart) if you haven't already, or jump straight into [the app guides](/guides/getting-around).
