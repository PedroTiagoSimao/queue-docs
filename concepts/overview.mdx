Before diving into features, it helps to understand how the app is structured. This overview explains the building blocks of the Google app and how they work together to support your day-to-day work.

## The core model

The app is organized around a simple hierarchy:

- **Workspace** — Your top-level environment. Everything you do lives inside your workspace.
- **Projects** — Collections of related work items, tasks, or content within a workspace.
- **Items** — The individual units of work: tasks, entries, records, or whatever applies to your use case.
- **Members** — The people with access to your workspace and projects.

Every feature in the app connects back to this model. When you create something, it belongs to a project. When you share something, you're giving workspace members access to it.

## How the app is organized

The interface is divided into three main areas:

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Sidebar** | Navigate between projects, settings, and sections |
| **Main panel** | View and interact with items, lists, and dashboards |
| **Top bar** | Search, notifications, and account access |

## Data and privacy

Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to you and the workspace members you invite. You can export your data at any time from **Settings → Data & Privacy**.

<Note>For details on how your data is handled, refer to the app's Privacy Policy linked in the footer.</Note>
