This glossary defines the terminology you'll encounter throughout the app and this help center. Refer back here whenever you come across an unfamiliar term.

## Core terms

<Accordion title="Workspace">
A workspace is your top-level environment in the app. It contains all your projects, members, and settings. Most users have one workspace, but the app supports multiple workspaces for different organizations or teams.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Project">
A project is a collection of related items organized around a common goal or theme. You can have as many projects as you need within a workspace. Projects can be private (visible only to you) or shared with workspace members.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Item">
An item is the fundamental unit of work in the app. Depending on your use case, an item might be a task, a record, a note, or an entry. Every item belongs to a project.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Member">
A member is anyone with access to your workspace. Members can be assigned different roles that control what they can see and do. See the Roles section below.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Role">
Roles determine what a workspace member can do. Common roles include:

- **Owner** — Full access; can manage billing, members, and workspace settings.
- **Admin** — Can manage projects and members but not billing.
- **Member** — Can create and edit items within projects they have access to.
- **Guest** — Read-only or limited access to specific projects.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Integration">
An integration is a connection between the app and an external tool or service (e.g., a calendar, communication platform, or file storage service). Integrations let you sync data and automate workflows across your tools.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Notification">
A notification is an alert that informs you about activity relevant to you — such as a comment mention, status change, or upcoming deadline. Notifications can appear in-app, via email, or as push alerts.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Dashboard">
The dashboard is your personalized home screen inside the app. It shows a summary of your most important items, recent activity, and quick-access links to your projects.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Activity Feed">
The activity feed is a chronological log of changes made to an item or project. It records who did what and when, making it easy to track progress and review history.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Label / Tag">
Labels (also called tags in some contexts) are customizable markers you apply to items to categorize or filter them. You can create your own labels and assign colors to them.
</Accordion>

| Role | Create & Edit | Manage Members | Manage Billing | Delete Workspace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Admin | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

<Note>Role permissions may vary depending on your plan. Check your workspace settings to see the permissions available on your current plan.</Note>
