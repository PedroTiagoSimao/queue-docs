This quickstart walks you through everything you need to do to go from a blank slate to actively using the app. Most users complete all steps in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Visit the app's sign-up page and enter your name, email address, and a secure password. You'll receive a verification email — click the link inside to activate your account.

    <Note>Check your spam folder if you don't see the verification email within a couple of minutes.</Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in for the first time">
    Once your account is verified, go to the login page and sign in with your email and password. You can also use a social login option (Google, Apple, or Microsoft) if your account supports it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    After logging in, you'll be prompted to fill in your profile details — your display name, profile photo, and any other information relevant to your use of the app. This step helps personalize your experience.

    You can always update your profile later from [Account Settings](/account/profile).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the dashboard">
    You're now on the main dashboard. From here you can:

    - Access all major features from the sidebar or top navigation
    - Start your first task, project, or item
    - Adjust your notification preferences
    - Connect any integrations you use

    Read the [Getting Around guide](/guides/getting-around) for a full tour of the interface.
  </Step>
</Steps>
