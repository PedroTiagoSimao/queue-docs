Notifications keep you informed about what's happening in your workspace — new comments, status changes, upcoming deadlines, and more. This guide explains how to view notifications and fine-tune them so you only get alerted about what matters to you.

## Viewing notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the top bar to open the notifications panel. Here you'll see:

- **Unread** — New alerts you haven't seen yet, highlighted in bold
- **All** — Your full notification history

Click any notification to jump directly to the relevant item, comment, or project.

## Notification types

The app can notify you about a range of events:

| Event | Default setting |
|---|---|
| Someone mentions you (`@you`) | ✅ On |
| An item assigned to you changes status | ✅ On |
| A due date is approaching (24 hrs before) | ✅ On |
| A comment is added to an item you follow | ✅ On |
| A new member joins your workspace | ❌ Off |
| A project you own is modified | ❌ Off |

## Configuring notification preferences

You can choose which events notify you and whether those alerts arrive in-app, by email, or as push notifications. Follow these steps to update your preferences:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Settings**, or press `G then S`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notifications">
    In the settings sidebar, click **Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your channels">
    For each notification type, choose whether to receive it **In-app**, **By email**, or **As a push notification**. You can toggle each channel independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a schedule (optional)">
    Under **Notification Schedule**, set the hours during which you want to receive notifications. Alerts outside those hours are held and delivered when your schedule resumes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email digests

Instead of individual email notifications, you can switch to a **daily digest** that summarizes all your activity in a single email. To enable it:

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications → Email**.
2. Toggle **Daily Digest** on.
3. Choose your preferred delivery time.

## Muting a project or item

If you're added to a busy project and don't want constant updates, you can mute it:

- **Mute a project** — Right-click the project in the sidebar and select **Mute notifications**.
- **Mute an item** — Open the item, click the three-dot menu, and select **Mute**.

<Tip>You can still view the item and project normally when muted — you just won't receive notifications about them.</Tip>

## Clearing notifications

To clear all notifications at once, open the notifications panel and click **Mark all as read** at the top right.
