## Live chat

Live chat is available directly inside the app:

1. Click the **chat bubble icon** in the bottom-right corner of any page.
2. Type your question or describe your issue.
3. A support agent will respond as quickly as possible.

**Availability:** Live chat agents are available Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm (your local time zone). Outside of these hours, you can still send a message and we'll reply by email.

## Email support

For non-urgent issues or when you need to attach files or screenshots:

- **Email:** support@example.com
- **Response time:** Within 1 business day for paid plans; within 3 business days for free plans.

When emailing, please include:
- Your account email address
- A clear description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the problem (if applicable)
- Screenshots or screen recordings (very helpful!)
- Your browser, OS, and app version

## Community forum

The community forum is a great place to:
- Ask questions and get answers from experienced users
- Share tips, workflows, and integrations
- Request features and vote on existing requests
- Browse solutions to common problems

Access the forum from **Help → Community** inside the app, or via the link in the app footer.

## Status page

If the app seems down or sluggish, check the **Status Page** before contacting support — an incident may already be in progress and our team may already be working on it.

The status page shows:
- Current system status (Operational / Degraded / Outage)
- Active incident updates in real time
- Historical uptime and past incidents

You can **subscribe to status updates** to receive an email or SMS whenever an incident is opened or resolved.

## Before you contact us

To help us resolve your issue faster, check these resources first:

- [Troubleshooting guide](/support/troubleshooting) — solutions to the most common problems
- [FAQ](/support/faq) — quick answers to frequent questions
- Use `Cmd+K` / `Ctrl+K` in the app to search for help articles

<Tip>The more detail you provide, the faster we can help. Screenshots and error messages are especially useful.</Tip>
